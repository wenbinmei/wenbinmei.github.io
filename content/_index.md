## What I do

- **Built teams from the ground up.** 6+ years as a people leader with up to 3 direct reports. Built a team of 10+ computational biologists and data analysts across the US and Ghent, Belgium, as Platform Manager, then led a cross-functional team of 8–9 computational biologists and ML scientists as Model Capability Lead.
- **Shipped models that matter.** Delivered AI models that prioritize gene targets and design multiplex edits, now powering Inari's Predictive Design Engine.
- **Connected science to the business.** Partner across R&D, product, and leadership to align computational strategy with business goals.
- **Track record.** Inventor on 8 patent applications and author of 19 peer-reviewed papers (2,500+ citations, h-index 18), from the maize W22 genome (*Nature Genetics*) to the *Amborella* genome (*Science*).

I am motivated by applying AI in biology to solve problems with real-world impact, from crops to human health.
